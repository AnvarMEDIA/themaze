# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: 23 April 2026_

This policy explains what cookies and similar technologies are used on
`www.maze.uz`.

## 1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser that remember your
preferences and help us understand how the Site is used.

## 2. Cookies we use

### Strictly necessary

- **Theme preference** — stores your dark/light choice.
- **Session cookie** (`maze_admin`) — only set after admin login; keeps
  you signed in.
- **Consent choice** — remembers whether you accepted analytics cookies.

### Analytics (with consent)

- **Yandex Metrika** — `_ym_uid`, `_ym_d`, `_ym_isad`. Aggregated usage
  analytics and session replay.
- **Vercel Analytics** — privacy-friendly, cookieless page view counting.
- **Vercel Speed Insights** — Core Web Vitals collection.

## 3. Managing your choice

When you first visit the site you will see a banner asking whether to
allow analytics cookies. You can change your choice any time from the
footer link **Cookie settings**, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

Declining analytics does not affect site functionality.

## 4. Third parties

Yandex and Vercel may process data outside your country. See their privacy
policies:
- Yandex: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/
- Vercel: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
