# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 April 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how **MAZE Studio** ("we", "us") collects, uses,
and protects personal data when you use our website `www.maze.uz`.

## 1. Who we are

MAZE Studio is a branding and design studio based in Tashkent, Uzbekistan.
For any privacy-related question, contact us at **hello@maze.uz**.

## 2. What we collect

We only collect data that you actively provide, plus minimal technical data
needed to operate the site securely.

- **Contact form submissions** — name, email, phone, company, service
  interest, budget, message. Used to respond to your inquiry. Stored until
  the project is completed or you ask us to delete it.
- **Server logs** — IP address, user agent, URL, timestamp. Used for rate
  limiting, debugging and security. Retained for 30 days.
- **Analytics** — aggregated usage statistics via Yandex Metrika and Vercel
  Analytics (page views, country, device). Anonymised. See our
  [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies) for detail and opt-out options.

## 3. Legal basis

We process your data based on your consent (you submit the form), our
legitimate interest in running the site, and compliance with applicable law
(UZ DP-Law No. ЗРУ-547 and, where applicable, GDPR).

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with:

- Service providers strictly required to deliver the site (Vercel for
  hosting, Resend for transactional email).
- Authorities, where we are legally obliged.

## 5. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data.
Email **hello@maze.uz** with the subject "Privacy request" and we will reply
within 30 days.

## 6. Retention

Inquiry data is kept for up to 36 months from last contact, then deleted
unless a contract obliges longer retention.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the
latest version.
